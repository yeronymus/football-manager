Community Rules & Match Guide

How match sign-ups, reserve promotions, rules, and MVP voting work.

1. How to Sign Up
  • Tap «⚽ Join Game» under the match announcement in the group.
  • The web interface opens directly inside Telegram.
  • First time? Complete the quick profile once (enter Latin name and select pitch positions).
  • Tap «Sign Up for Game». Open spots put you straight into the Main Squad. When full, you enter the Reserve Queue.
Tap any screenshot to enlarge
2. Moving from Reserve to Main Squad

When a main squad player drops out, the bot automatically promotes the first reserve player into the main roster.

The bot sends a direct Telegram message notification. Keep bot notifications unmuted.
3. Cancellation Policy

To cancel: open the match card in the bot and tap «❌ Leave Game».

  • Free cancellation: cancel at least 24 hours before kick-off so reserve players have time to prepare.
  • Late cancellation (< 24 hours): no fee is charged for late cancellation, only a yellow card (🟨).
4. Match Fee & Payment

The fee and bank account details are shown on the match card. Please send the payment before the match via bank transfer to the provided account.

5. Pitch Conduct & Disciplinary Cards
  • Dangerous tackles, rough challenges, fights, and toxic insults are strictly prohibited.
  • Arrive on time to avoid losing booked pitch time.
  • Yellow Card (🟨): warning issued for unsportsmanlike conduct, late arrival, or late cancellation (< 24h). Active for 2–4 weeks.
  • Red Card (🟥): issued for 2 yellow cards, gross misconduct, or unexcused no-shows. Temporarily suspends match registration.
  • Card Reason: the specific reason is always stated in the card notification and in your profile. If you have questions or want to discuss a card, you can always contact the organizers below.
6. Post-Match & MVP Voting

The MVP poll opens in the group chat after full-time. Vote for 1 player from EACH team (1 from Green + 1 from Orange = 2 votes total). Do not vote for only 1 player overall.

7. Profile, Stats & Active Cards

Personal statistics, match history, and MMR rating are available anytime via /profile in private chat with the bot. Your active cards, the reason they were issued, and the exact expiration date / remaining days are also displayed directly on your profile card.
